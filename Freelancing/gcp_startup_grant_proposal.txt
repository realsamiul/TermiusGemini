### PART 1: THE TWO CHANNELS — VALUE PROPOSITION & TECH STACK

#### CHANNEL A: DocRAG-Legal (Enterprise Regulatory Compliance Auditor)
* **The Emerging Market Friction**: In developing economies like Bangladesh, 
  corporate operations, financial liquidity, and trade flows are governed by 
  a labyrinth of highly volatile, frequently shifting central bank (Bangladesh Bank) 
  and revenue board (NBR) circulars. Missing a compliance update results in severe 
  financial litigation, blocked trade transactions, and corporate fines.
* **The Technical Breakthrough**: DocRAG-Legal V2 is a fully automated corporate 
  and contract risk auditor. 
  - *Serverless Layout-Aware Retrieval*: Uses standard Discovery Engine Search 
    (`discoveryengine.googleapis.com`) with OCR layouts active to crawl, parse, 
    and index dense, multi-column legal acts, tariffs, and financial spreadsheets.
  - *Adversarial Self-Audit*: Every generated answer is audited by a custom 
    advisory judge (`eval_judge.py`) running on Gemini, scoring faithfulness and 
    flagging specific statutory gaps before the user ever renders the payload.
  - *Chained Cryptographic Ledger*: Every session committed to the Modal Ndjson 
    storage layer is signed with a SHA256 provenance hash chain (prev_hash + 
    query + answer + context), proving the audit history is completely tamper-evident 
    and secure for institutional boardrooms.

#### CHANNEL B: Erudite (Empathetic Admissions & Aid Compliance Auditor)
* **The Regional Opportunity**: Brilliant, high-need South Asian and Bangladeshi 
  students are completely locked out of global elite education due to a total lack 
  of high-quality college counseling and severe systemic information asymmetry

* **The Technical Breakthrough**: Erudite V2 is a personalized, highly empathetic 
  admissions compliance auditor.
  - *Structured "Soothing" Output*: Instead of dumping walls of anxiety-inducing 
    text, the API returns a structured JSON payload separating warm counselor greetings,

  - *The Compassionate Counselor Voice*: Through advanced system preambles,
