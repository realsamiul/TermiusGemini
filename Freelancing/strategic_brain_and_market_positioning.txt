### PART 1: THE CENTRAL REASONING ENGINE — THE AGNOSTIC BRAIN

The architecture of DocRAG-Legal V2 is fundamentally a **Domain-Agnostic Core 
Reasoning Brain**. By separating the "processing machinery" from the "data context," 
the system achieves massive flexibility with zero code contortion:

```
                  [AGNOSTIC PROCESSING MACHINERY]
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Retrieval Plane  ──►  Adversarial Judge  ──►  Modal Ledger  │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                 ▲
                     [MUTABLE DOMAIN CONTEXTS]
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Tax Codes  │  Banking Liquidity  │  Import/Export  │  Laws  │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Every single component we have deployed remains identical across use cases:
1. **The Retrieval Wrapper**: Performs standard semantic searches. It does not 
   care if it is pulling contract clauses, tax articles, or banking liquidity circulars.
2. **The Adversarial Evaluator (`eval_judge`)**: Assesses if the generated 
   synthesis is faithful to the retrieved chunks. Its safety logic (Score, Gaps, 
   Citations, and Ambiguity challenges) is mathematically universal.
3. **The Ledger Registry (Modal)**: Computes the immutable provenance SHA256 chain. 
   Chaining previous session states to prevent tampering is fully domain-independent.

**Strategic Benefit**: To pivot this entire backend from corporate contract analysis 
to Bangladeshi tax compliance, you do **not** rewrite the software. You simply 
point the backend to a different Discovery Engine data store (`tax-search-store`) and 
adjust the synthesis preamble. The core "brain" remains completely unchanged.

### PART 2: THE "ZONED QUERY" FREEMIUM LEAD GENERATION HOOK

To scale this platform as a high-growth B2B SaaS, you must offer an incredibly frictionless 
landing page experience ("The Zoned Query") that captures users, leaving the compute-intensive, 
high-value document uploads for paying corporate accounts.

#### 1. The Zoned Query (Free Tier / Lead Capture)
* **How it works**: The user lands on a beautiful, minimalist landing page. They 
  do **not** upload files. Instead, they type general questions into "Zoned Areas" 
  loaded with public legal/financial repositories (e.g. "What is the tax rate on 
  foreign software import?" or "What are Bangladesh Bank's current SLR reserves?").
* **Technical Backing**: The frontend routes these queries directly to your standard, 
  pre-provisioned public data stores (`statutes-search-store`, `banking-circulars-store`) 
  using the standard Search API.
* **Why it works**: **Cost is near-zero**. Standard search under your $1,000 credit 
  absorbs the cost entirely, giving users instant, highly precise, grounded answers 
  with no card burn.

#### 2. The Heavy-Duty Document Auditor (Premium Paying Tier)
* **How it works**: Once a corporate user experiences the accuracy of the free tier, 
  they upgrade to the premium tier to audit their own **private contracts, audit sheets, 
  and balance ledgers**.
* **Technical Backing**: This unlocks active `/risk` audits, verbatim `/redline` 
  rewriting, and private, custom GCS bucket ingestion pipelines.
* **Why it works**: The client pays a high monthly subscription fee to offset private 
  Gemini 1.5 Pro processing costs, creating a highly profitable, self-funding SaaS model.

### PART 3: TARGETED HIGH-IMPACT DISCOPLINES IN BANGLADESH

In Bangladesh, legal and regulatory frameworks hold disproportionate authority over 
corporate operations and private assets. Expanding your data stores into these 
specific areas will instantly turn the heads of boardroom executives and high-net-worth individuals:

#### A. Banking & Financial Liquidity Compliance (Enterprise Focus)
* **The Context**: Bangladesh Bank (the central bank) frequently issues highly complex, 
  binding circulars governing Cash Reserve Ratios (CRR), Statutory Liquidity Ratios (SLR), 
  foreign exchange transactions, and non-performing loan (NPL) classifications.
* **Active Asset**: Create a `banking-compliance-store`. Ingest all recent Bangladesh Bank 
  circulars. Corporate CFOs can query this store to audit their weekly liquidity 
  reports against central bank mandates instantly.

#### B. Customs, Tariffs, & Import-Export Customs (SME/Trade Focus)
* **The Context**: Importing raw materials and exporting finished goods in Bangladesh 
  is subject to a labyrinth of National Board of Revenue (NBR) customs acts, import 
  tariffs, customs clearance regulations, and Letter of Credit (LC) procedures.
* **Active Asset**: Create an `import-export-store`. Ingest NBR customs acts, HS tariff codes, 
  and export policies. Traders can paste their LC terms to flag compliance risk and 
  optimize duties.

#### C. Sharia & Civil Family Inheritance Law (Consumer B2C Focus)
* **The Context**: Distribution of estate assets in Bangladesh is subject to incredibly 
  complex Muslim Sharia inheritance laws (Faraiz), Hindu personal law, and civil partition 
  procedures, notoriously causing decades of family litigation.
* **Active Asset**: Create an `inheritance-law-store`. Ingest standard inheritance acts, 
  distribution matrices, and landmark court precedents. Users can query the exact shares 
  allotted to heirs based on family structures.

### PART 4: DISCOVERY ENGINE ADVANCED FACETS FOR SAAS INTEGRITY

To maximize the performance, quality, and user experience of these data stores, 
we can configure three advanced Discovery Engine platform facets in the console:

#### A. Document Layout OCR & Parsing Tuning
* *The Facet*: Under Data Store settings, configure the **Advanced OCR parser**. 
  This ensures that when auditing multi-column tax sheets, tabular financial statements, 
  or complex central bank liquidity tables, the OCR reads cells and row alignments 
  without tearing numbers away from their headers.

#### B. Autocomplete & Suggested Query Collections
* *The Facet*: Enable the **Autocomplete and Suggested Queries** engine in Agent Builder. 
  As a student or trader types, the system suggests verified legal terms (e.g., typing "mat" 
  suggests "maternity benefits under Section 45"), dramatically improving UX search friction.

#### C. Document Schema Mapping & User Segments
* *The Facet*: Ingest companion JSONL files with your PDFs. Map metadata schemas (e.g., 
  `category: "sharia_inheritance"`, `year: "2024"`, `risk_level: "high"`). This allows our 
  FastAPI backend to pass precise, hyper-focused segment filters on the fly, accelerating 
  relevance and eliminating out-of-scope retrieval.
