# Amherst College Forensic Intelligence Report for a High-Need Bangladeshi Applicant

## Executive Summary

Amherst College is a **genuinely fundable** U.S. option for a high-need Bangladeshi undergraduate applicant, not a symbolic-aid school. On Amherst’s international-specific pages, the college says it is **need-blind for international applicants**, **meets 100% of calculated financial need**, and requires **no loans in the initial package**. In Amherst’s 2024–2025 Common Data Set Section H6, the college reported **135** aided nonresident alien undergraduates, an **average institutional award of $66,093**, and **$8,922,544** in total institutional aid to nonresident aliens. For a student who truly needs **$55,000–$75,000 per year**, Amherst is therefore a school where the money can plausibly work **if admission happens**. The main barrier is admission itself: Amherst’s official fast-facts page reports a **9% overall admission rate**. All cited web sources in this report were retrieved on **2026-05-21**. citeturn0search0turn3search13turn3search3turn5search9

The main caution is **not** that Amherst fails to fund internationals. The caution is that Amherst’s public package descriptions do **not reconcile cleanly**. Its international applicants page advertises **“83% international students with aid”** and an **“$82,000+ average international financial aid award”** for 2024–2025, while the standardised CDS H6 figure for institutional aid is **$66,093**. One possible partial explanation is that Amherst’s broader aid messaging may count package elements differently than CDS H6, because Amherst’s general aid examples include campus employment, whereas H6 reports institutional aid. Still, the accessible official materials reviewed here do not directly explain the gap. citeturn3search13turn3search3turn0search7

For an unhooked Bangladeshi applicant with no special talent or institutional hook assumed, Amherst should be treated as a **high-reach, high-reward full-aid lottery**: unusually strong on aid reality, extremely difficult on admission. citeturn5search9turn0search0turn3search3

For a Bangladeshi applicant applying under **Regular Decision** and needing very large aid, Amherst is therefore an academically elite long shot, even though the aid policy itself is unusually strong. citeturn6search12turn5search9turn3search13
